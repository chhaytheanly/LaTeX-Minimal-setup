In this chapter, I will present the novel methodologies and techniques employed in the development of the edge-AI framework for real-time detection, classification, and tallying of metallic fasteners on industrial conveyors. The chapter is structured to provide a comprehensive overview of the research design, data collection, model architecture, training procedures, deployment strategies, and evaluation metrics.
In order to ensure readability and clarity, the chapter is organized into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the methodology. The first section outlines the research design and objectives, followed by a detailed description of the data collection process, including the creation of a purpose-built dataset of fastener images captured under realistic conveyor conditions. 
Subsequent sections delve into the model architecture, highlighting the novel EMAc2f module and its components, as well as the training procedures employed to optimize model performance. The deployment strategies section discusses the integration of the trained model into an NVIDIA Jetson embedded platform with TensorRT acceleration, while the evaluation metrics section presents the criteria used to assess model accuracy and efficiency. Finally, the chapter concludes with a discussion of the limitations and potential future directions for this research.
